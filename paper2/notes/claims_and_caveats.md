# Claims and caveats

## Allowed claims
- Phase conditioning removes a substantial finite-range component of lag-one covariance.
- The residual remains negative in the tested windows.
- ABCD closure is an exact numerical consistency check for the decomposition.
- The 10^10 window reproduces frozen predictions and validates scale transfer over the tested range.

## Avoid
- “The phase explains all dependence.”
- “The residual converges to zero.”
- “The result proves a Hardy–Littlewood or Montgomery–Soundararajan law.”
- “The phase decomposition is a new theory of prime gaps.”
- “Murray's method is wrong” or “this is a Comment on Murray.”

## Theory language
Use “conditional heuristic,” “finite-range consistency check,” and “empirical decomposition.”
