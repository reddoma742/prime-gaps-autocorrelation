We study the lag-one covariance of consecutive prime gaps $g_n = p_{n+1}-p_n$ in the window $[10^{10}, 3\times 10^{10})$, after local detrending and conditioning on the opening-prime residue modulo a primorial $M$. For each of seven moduli $M \in \{6, 30, 210, 2310, 30030, 510510, 9699690\}$ we obtain an exact decomposition $A+X+Y+D=\operatorname{Cov}$ that closes to within $10^{-6}$. The residual correlation $\rho_{\text{resid}}$ remains negative at all seven scales, decreasing in magnitude from $-0.0247$ to $-0.0103$. The between-phase variance grows monotonically with $M$, accounting for up to $3.74\%$ of $\operatorname{Var}(e)$. The non-$D$ terms account for $60.4\%$ of the lag-one covariance at the largest tested modulus, while between-phase structure accounts for $3.74\%$ of the one-point variance. We report block-bootstrap $95\%$ intervals for $\rho_{\text{resid}}$ that exclude zero at every scale. The results are finite-range and do not claim asymptotic laws.
