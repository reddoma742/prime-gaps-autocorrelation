# 9. Relation to Murray

Murray studies recurrence statistics of consecutive-prime residue classes under CRT-aligned primorial lifts, using finite-state transition models and projections back to a coarse modulus. Our observable is different: we study the lag-one covariance of numerical prime-gap sizes after local detrending, conditioning on the opening-prime residue modulo a primorial. The two approaches are complementary: both resolve hidden divisibility coordinates, but they address different observables and make different statistical claims.

No numerical comparison with Murray's results is made in this paper. The purpose of this section is to record the difference of observable, not to evaluate or extend Murray's claims.

A note on terminology. Murray's second paper uses the term "covariance" for the resolution covariance of tuple densities under primorial lifts. In the present paper, "covariance" refers throughout to the statistical lag-one covariance of consecutive gap sizes after local detrending. The two usages are unrelated.
